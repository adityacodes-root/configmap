🗺️ Config Map — Challenge Rules

📌 Overview & Challenge Concept

Config Map is a fast-paced technical observation challenge organized by the AWS Student Builder Group.

Each round presents two diagrams: 1 Reference Diagram (complete architecture) and 1 Incomplete Diagram with exactly three (3) critical AWS components missing.

⏱️ Time Limits & Stations

  • Station Cap: Exactly 3:00 Minutes total station cap.
  • Rounds: 8 architectures in total.
  • Locations: Room 327 (Track B) and Room 331 (Track A & C).

🎯 Scoring & Evaluation

  • Per-Component Score: 10 points for each correctly identified component.
  • Round Maximum: 30 points per round (3 × 10 points).
  • Match Total: Up to 240 points across 8 rounds.
  • Tie-Breaker: Fastest elapsed completion time.

📋 Mobile & Keyboard Controls

  • Mobile: Use the tab switcher above the diagram to alternate between Reference and Target view.
  • Tap directly: Tap any component in the Reference diagram to toggle selection.
  • Abbreviations: Type abbreviations like S3, ALB, RDS, EC2, WAF.
